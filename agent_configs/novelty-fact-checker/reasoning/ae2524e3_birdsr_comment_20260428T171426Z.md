## Paper evidence

The ablation subsection in `sec/4_experiment.tex` defines four training settings:

1. `Only paired forward`: synthetic paired forward optimization.
2. `Only real-world LR reverse`: reverse reward optimization only for real-world low-resolution images.
3. `All reverse`: reverse optimization for both synthetic and real-world low-resolution inputs.
4. `Mixed forward and reverse`: the full Bird-SR bidirectional framework.

Table 2 reports these settings on RealSR:

- Setting 1: MUSIQ 66.687, LPIPS 0.344, training cost 22%.
- Setting 2: MUSIQ 67.097, LPIPS 0.347, training cost 100%.
- Setting 3: MUSIQ 67.125, LPIPS 0.328, training cost 100%.
- Setting 4: MUSIQ 67.257, LPIPS 0.322, training cost 64%.

This means the real-world LR reverse path is not absent from the ablation. However, the full method's quality gain over all-reverse on RealSR is modest: MUSIQ rises by 0.132 and LPIPS improves by 0.006. A major argument for the mixed scheme is therefore also cost/stability, not only a large quality improvement.

The loss-component ablation in the supplement for DiT4SR reports:

- Reward-only setting A: ClipIQA 0.541, LPIPS 0.355.
- Full reward + semantic + structural setting: ClipIQA 0.565, LPIPS 0.322.

This supports the claim that semantic and structural constraints reduce the risk of pure perceptual reward optimization degrading structure.

The abstract states that Bird-SR "consistently outperforms state-of-the-art methods in perceptual quality while preserving structural consistency." Table 1 is narrower than that claim:

- On RealSR, SeeSR has better LPIPS, MUSIQ, and LIQE than DiT4SR with Bird-SR, while Bird-SR is best on MANIQA among listed methods.
- On DRealSR, other external methods have better LPIPS or MUSIQ than DiT4SR with Bird-SR, although Bird-SR improves its own backbone and is best/comparable on several perceptual metrics.
- On RealLR200 and RealLQ250, DiT4SR with Bird-SR is often best or near best among listed methods.

The safer empirical claim is that Bird-SR consistently improves the chosen diffusion SR backbones and is best or comparable across several perceptual metrics, not that it uniformly dominates all state-of-the-art methods on all reported metrics.
